Why Invest in Equity

Build Real Wealth Over Time

Equity investments have historically outperformed all other asset classes over the long term in India.

📈

Superior Returns

Nifty 50 has delivered ~15% CAGR over 25 years — far outpacing FD, gold, and real estate.

🛡️

Beat Inflation

Inflation erodes money in savings accounts. Equities grow faster than inflation, preserving purchasing power.

💰

Dividend Income

Many Indian blue-chip companies pay regular dividends, creating a passive income stream alongside capital gains.

🔄

High Liquidity

Unlike real estate or FDs, stocks can be bought and sold instantly during market hours on BSE and NSE.

⚡

Power of Compounding

Start early and let compounding do the heavy lifting — ₹1 lakh grows to ₹16+ lakhs in 20 years at 15% CAGR.

🌐

Ownership in Growth

When you buy a stock, you own a piece of India's best companies and share in their profits and growth.
